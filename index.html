Frequently Asked Questions

What is University Notes?
University Notes is a comprehensive educational platform providing students with easy access to lecture materials, study guides, and past examination papers through a streamlined Android experience.
Is the APK safe to install?
Yes, our APK is 100% safe and verified. It doesn't require any unnecessary permissions. Always ensure you download it from this official website to stay protected.
How do I install the APK?
Once downloaded, tap the file in your notification bar. If prompted, enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in your Android settings. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish the installation.
How do I install the Web App on iOS or Desktop?
For iOS users, open the Web App in Safari, tap the 'Share' icon, and select 'Add to Home Screen'. For Desktop users (Chrome/Edge), look for the 'Install' icon in the address bar or select 'Install App' from the browser menu. This allows you to access University Notes just like a native application.
How often is the app updated?
We update our content database weekly and the application itself monthly to introduce new features and performance improvements.
Can I use University Notes on iPhone or my Laptop?
Yes! While we offer a native Android APK for the best mobile experience, iPhone and Laptop users can access the full platform via our Web App at webuni.clastarhub.com.
What is "Six AI"?
Six AI is our built-in academic assistant designed to help you summarize long notes, explain complex concepts, and answer study-related questions instantly.
How do I get a verified badge (Blue Tick)?
Verification is granted to trusted contributors and official student accounts. You can apply for a badge by submitting your student ID for review through the profile settings in the app.
Can I chat with other students?
Absolutely. Our platform includes a Community Center where you can follow other students, join group discussions, and send direct messages for academic collaboration.
Is there a subscription fee?
No. Our core library of lecture notes and past papers is completely free. We believe in accessible education for all students.

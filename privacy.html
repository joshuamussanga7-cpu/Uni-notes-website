Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to University Notes. We are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

  • Profile Information: Name, email, course, and year of study.
  • Identity Data: Student ID photos for verification (Encrypted and stored securely).
  • Activity Data: 'Last Active' status to provide community features.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • Verification: Reviewing IDs to grant the Blue Tick verification badge.
  • Security: Detecting bots and ensuring a safe platform.
  • Community: Displaying your profile in the community sections.

4. DATA SECURITY

We use Firebase Security Rules and advanced encryption to protect your data. We do NOT sell your information to third parties.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

You can edit or delete your data anytime. Account deletion permanently removes all personal data.

6. CONTACT

For queries, visit the Support section or contact Clastar Technologies.
